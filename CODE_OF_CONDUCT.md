# Code of conduct

ArchiveBind welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds, identities, and experience levels. Participation in this project means agreeing to help keep its spaces respectful, safe, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and specific when giving or receiving technical feedback.
- Discuss ideas and observable behavior rather than attacking people or assuming motives.
- Welcome questions, acknowledge mistakes, and accept correction gracefully.
- Respect authors, readers, maintainers, and the communities served by supported sites.
- Protect privacy: use synthetic examples and sanitized diagnostics when seeking help.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, insults, or sexualized attention.
- Publishing or soliciting another person’s private information.
- Posting downloaded story text, private account material, cookies, credentials, access tokens, or sensitive URLs.
- Encouraging challenge bypasses, account abuse, excessive automated traffic, copyright infringement, or evasion of a creator’s access choices.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, impersonation, or repeated bad-faith argument after a decision has been explained.

## Scope

This policy applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, release/support conversations, and public spaces where someone is representing ArchiveBind or its community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, code, issues, or other contributions that violate this policy. Depending on severity and history, responses may include a private correction, warning, temporary participation restriction, or permanent ban. Immediate action may be taken for threats, targeted harassment, credential exposure, or publication of private material.

## Reporting

Use GitHub’s repository moderation tools to report abusive content. For a concern involving a maintainer or information that should not be posted publicly, use the private contact method listed on the repository owner’s GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Good-faith reports will be reviewed without retaliation and handled as confidentially as the platform and a fair investigation reasonably permit. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this policy.
