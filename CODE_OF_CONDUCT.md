# Code of conduct

ArchiveBind welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than people, accept correction gracefully, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, or publishing another person’s private information. Respect authors and never post downloaded story contents or private account material while seeking support.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner’s GitHub profile or repository moderation tools. Good-faith reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
